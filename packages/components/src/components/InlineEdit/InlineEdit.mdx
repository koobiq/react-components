## Behavior

- Click, Enter and Space open the editor.
- Enter and outside click save locally; Escape restores the value captured on open.
- In a textarea, Enter inserts a newline and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter saves.
- Tab and Shift+Tab at a panel boundary save and move focus in that direction; an adjacent enabled InlineEdit opens automatically.
- `onSaved` is a local event, not an asynchronous persistence handler.
- Programmatic mode changes do not emit `onSaved` or `onCanceled`.
- MVP view content must not contain links, buttons or other interactive descendants.
- MVP does not provide validation, background save, action buttons or menus.
